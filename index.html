About MooCrop

MooCrop is a free, no-account image cropping tool that runs entirely in your web browser. There's nothing to install and nothing to sign up for — pick a photo, drag the crop box, and download the result. Because the cropping happens on your own device using the browser's built-in Canvas API, your photos are never uploaded to a server, which makes MooCrop a fast option for cropping anything you'd rather keep private, like ID photos, screenshots, or personal pictures.

How it works

Drop one or more photos onto the page, or tap "Choose photo(s)" to browse your device. Once a photo loads, drag the crop box to reposition it, or pull the corner handles to resize. You can switch between a free-form rectangle, a locked square, or a circle, and snap to common aspect ratios like 1:1, 4:3, or 16:9 for social media posts, profile pictures, or print sizes. Rotate or flip the image, nudge brightness and contrast, then set an exact pixel width and height if you need a precise output size. When you're happy with the result, hit Download — MooCrop exports a PNG (with transparency for circle crops) or JPG straight to your device. If you loaded several photos, they're bundled into a single ZIP so you don't have to download each one separately.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Every crop, rotation, and adjustment happens locally in your browser. Your images are never sent to MooCrop's servers or any third party as part of the cropping process.

What image formats can I use?

You can upload common formats like JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF, and export the cropped result as a PNG or JPG.

Can I crop more than one photo at a time?

Yes. Select multiple files and MooCrop will let you adjust and crop each one, then bundle them into a single ZIP download.

Is there a size or quality limit?

MooCrop preserves your source image's resolution unless you set a custom output size, and exports PNGs losslessly. Very large batches depend on your device's available memory since everything is processed locally.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no email, no sign-up. Just open the page and start cropping.
